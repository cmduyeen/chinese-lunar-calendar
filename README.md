## Subscribe by link

Copy this URL, choose **Subscribe by URL** or **From URL** in your calendar app, then paste the link and save:

```text
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cmduyeen/chinese-lunar-calendar/main/output/chinese_lunar_latest.ics
```

Subscribe to the URL instead of downloading the `.ics` file so your calendar app can receive updates. Compatible with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, and other iCalendar apps.

## Calendar contents

- Daily Chinese lunar dates, including leap months.
- Lunar New Year's Eve, Spring Festival, Lantern Festival, Dragon Heads-raising Day, Dragon Boat Festival, Qixi Festival, Ghost Festival, Mid-Autumn Festival, Double Ninth Festival, Laba Festival, and Xiaonian.
- Qingming Festival, determined by China Standard Time.

The calendar uses China Standard Time (`Asia/Shanghai`), is updated annually, and covers the next 10 years. It lists lunar dates and traditional festivals, not official public-holiday or make-up workday schedules.
